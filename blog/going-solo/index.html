    Going solo, in public

    Noaman KhalilFounder of Jabble
    Ask AIChatGPT·Claude·Perplexity·AI Mode·GrokMarkdown

    My Mac apps

    • BondiWhy your Mac is slow, in one sentence
    • WiggleThe assistant at your cursor

    Quick answer

    I am building indie Mac apps under Jabble, in public, from San Francisco. The two you can look at today are Bondi and Wiggle. Jabble.io is the marketing toolkit I shipped because I was tired of paying for QR codes.

    On October 20 I wrote it down in public. After years of product work, I am building the apps myself.

    The long version of the résumé is ordinary and specific. Product at Apple, Tesla, TOPPAN Security, Bayut and Dubizzle. Before that, Namshi and HTC Vive. A bachelor’s from SAE in Dubai, and a master’s from Westcliff. None of that is a studio. Jabble is the studio.

    Jabble Super Intelligence is where the Mac apps live. Bondi tells you why a Mac is slow, in one sentence, and checks every number. Wiggle shows up at the cursor when you shake the mouse. A third Mac app has no name yet. I will describe it when there is something to show.

    Jabble.io is the other thread: QR codes, short links, bio pages, file transfer, and campaign analytics. I built it because I was tired of paying for tools a small business should not have to rent.

    I am in San Francisco. Email is the reliable way to reach me. I read hi@noamankhalil.com.
