# Introducing Bondi

By Noaman Khalil · Oct 3, 2026 · Updated Nov 1, 2026

Bondi is a native macOS system monitor in alpha. It groups helper processes under the app that started them and writes one sentence about why the Mac is slow. Bondi measures every number. The on-device model only writes the words, and a wrong number is thrown out. Launch is Tuesday, February 2, 2027.
