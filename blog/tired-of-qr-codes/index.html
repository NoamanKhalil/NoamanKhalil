    I got tired of paying for QR codes

    Noaman KhalilFounder of Jabble
    Ask AIChatGPT·Claude·Perplexity·AI Mode·GrokMarkdown

    Quick answer

    Jabble.io does QR code menus, file transfer, short links, bio pages, and campaign analytics. I built it because rented QR tools added up. Most of the useful parts cost nothing to start.

    I was tired of paying for QR codes, so I built Jabble.

    A café menu, a file you need to hand someone across a counter, a short link for a campaign, a bio page that does not belong to yet another login. Those are ordinary jobs. The invoices for them are not ordinary once you add them up. Jabble.io is the toolkit I wanted on one site: QR code menus, file transfer, short links, bio pages, and campaign analytics.

    Most of the useful parts are free. That is the point. If you run a small business, you should be able to put a menu on a table without a subscription lecture.

    Jabble.io is separate from the Mac apps. Bondi and Wiggle live under jabble.si. Same person, different jobs. When I write about either one here, I am writing about my own product. Treat that as the conflict of interest it is.
