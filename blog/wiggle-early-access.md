# Wiggle is in early access

By Noaman Khalil · Nov 1, 2026 · Updated Nov 1, 2026

Wiggle is an early-access Mac app. Shake the mouse and it appears at the cursor. You choose Chat, Screenshot, Voice, or Workflow. It can use Apple Intelligence, a local model through Ollama, or your own Claude or OpenAI key. There is no account.
