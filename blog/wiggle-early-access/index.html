    Wiggle is in early access

    Noaman KhalilFounder of Jabble
    Ask AIChatGPT·Claude·Perplexity·AI Mode·GrokMarkdown

    My Mac apps

    • BondiWhy your Mac is slow, in one sentence
    • WiggleThe assistant at your cursor

    Quick answer

    Wiggle is an early-access Mac app. Shake the mouse and it appears at the cursor. You choose Chat, Screenshot, Voice, or Workflow. It can use Apple Intelligence, a local model through Ollama, or your own Claude or OpenAI key. There is no account.

    Wiggle is the other Mac app. This note is where it stands on November 1, 2026. It is in early access. I am not calling today a launch day.

    The idea is small. You are stuck on something already on screen. You should not have to leave that spot, open a browser, and explain the picture again. Shake the mouse, five quick strokes, and Wiggle appears at the cursor. Slow drags do not count.

    Each time it opens you pick one of four things. The keys are C, S, V, and W.

    • Chat. Ask in writing about whatever is on screen.
    • Screenshot. A screenshot happens when you choose it. It is not taken for you in the background.
    • Voice. Ask out loud. Voice is a mode you pick, the same way you pick Chat.
    • Workflow. Record clicks, typing, and shortcuts, then replay them. A workflow stays on the Mac.

    The model is your choice. On a Mac that has Apple Intelligence, Wiggle can use Apple’s on-device model. You can point it at a local model you already run through Ollama. Or you can bring your own Claude or OpenAI key, and that question goes to the provider you picked, because you asked it to.

    There is no account, and the app does not ship with analytics. A workflow is stored on the Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Wiggle/workflows.json, and is not uploaded. macOS withholds keystrokes typed into password fields, so those are never recorded.

    Wiggle is a product of Jabble. Early access is the right word for it until I have a public build and a price I am willing to print here. When that changes, this page gets a new date.
